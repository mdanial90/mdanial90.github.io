1. Executive Summary & Strategic Rationale

Recommendation. Establish a single, Board-anchored Group Head of Internal Audit (GHIA) at Senior Leadership grade, reporting functionally to the Board Audit Committee (BAC) and administratively to the Group CEO, with solid-line authority over all port OpCo audit teams. Recruit at a market-competitive total cash package of ~RM 0.85m–1.15m p.a. (midpoint ~RM 0.95m), targeting appointment within 4–6 months.

1.1 Why the role must be centralised and strengthened — now

Scale & concentration of value

MMC Ports is Malaysia's largest port operator, handling the majority of the country's container and a large share of conventional/bulk throughput through PTP, Northport, Johor Port, Penang Port, Tanjung Bruas and associated terminals. Multi-billion-ringgit concession assets and licences depend on demonstrable control.

Capital-markets readiness

Any listing or capital-raising of the port platform requires Bursa Main Market LR para 15.27 compliance (an independent internal audit function reporting to the audit committee), MCCG 2021 Principle B practices, and a defensible statement on risk management & internal control.

Corporate liability — MACC Act s.17A

Since June 2020 the commercial organisation itself, and its directors/management, are liable for corruption by associated persons unless “adequate procedures” are proven. Ports have dense exposure points: customs, berthing priority, procurement, land & licensing, labour contractors.

CapEx & automation wave

Terminal expansion, yard automation, crane replacement and digital port systems create large, multi-year contracts where cost overrun, variation-order abuse and bid collusion are the dominant value-leak risks.

IT/OT cyber convergence

TOS, gate automation, OCR, crane PLCs and port community systems are designated critical infrastructure (NCII under the Cyber Security Act 2024). A single OT incident can halt transshipment flows.

Fragmented assurance today

Historically OpCo-centric audit teams with differing methodologies, ratings and tools produce inconsistent assurance, duplicated coverage, and no consolidated view of risk for the Board.

2.3 Job description

Job purpose. To provide the Board, through the Board Audit Committee, with independent, objective and risk-based assurance and insight on the effectiveness of governance, risk management and internal control across MMC Port Holdings Berhad and all its port operating companies, joint ventures and projects — protecting the Group's assets, concessions, licences and reputation, and helping management improve operational and capital efficiency.

Core accountability buckets

  1. Risk-based audit strategy & planning. Maintain the group audit universe and a rolling 3-year strategy and annual plan aligned to the enterprise risk register, strategic plan and BAC risk appetite; ensure coverage of all OpCos, JVs, key processes, CapEx and IT/OT; dynamically re-plan for emerging risk.
  2. Group & OpCo audit governance. Own the Internal Audit Charter, methodology, rating scale and QAIP; lead and develop OpCo audit spokes; manage resources, budget and co-source panel; secure an external quality assessment at least every five years (conformance with IIA Standards).
  3. Fraud, integrity & investigations (MACC s.17A). Independently test the anti-bribery and corruption programme (ABMS / ISO 37001 where adopted) against the T.R.U.S.T. principles; lead or oversee whistleblowing-triggered investigations; coordinate with Group Integrity, Legal and, where required, MACC and the Police; report outcomes to BAC/Board.
  4. CapEx, procurement & contract assurance. Provide pre-award, in-flight and post-completion audits of major projects (terminal expansion, cranes, automation, dredging); test tender integrity, bid-rigging indicators, variation orders, liquidated damages, and contractor claims; review concession-agreement compliance.
  5. Digital, data-led & continuous auditing. Build an analytics capability on ERP, TOS, billing, payroll and gate data; deploy continuous control monitoring (CCM), process mining and AI-assisted exception testing; deliver IT general control, cybersecurity and OT security audits aligned to the IIA Cybersecurity Topical Requirement and NCII obligations.
  6. Board reporting, stakeholder assurance & advisory. Report quarterly to the BAC on plan delivery, significant findings, overdue actions, fraud cases and the GHIA's overall opinion on internal control; support the Statement on Risk Management & Internal Control; coordinate combined assurance with risk, compliance, ESG/sustainability assurance, parent-company audit and external auditors.

Operational scope

  • Entities: MMC Port Holdings Berhad and all subsidiaries (PTP, Northport, Johor Port, Penang Port, Tanjung Bruas Port and associated terminals, cruise terminals, marine and logistics services) plus audit rights in JVs and associates.
  • Function size & budget: ~35–55 FTE; annual opex ~RM 12–18m incl. co-source and tools (indicative).
  • Authority: unrestricted access to records, systems, property and personnel; private sessions with the BAC at least twice a year without management present.

3.3 Candidate specification profile

Mandatory

  • Degree in Accounting, Finance, Engineering or related.
  • Chartered Accountant — MIA member (CA(M)) and/or ACCA, CPA (Aust/US), ICAEW, CIMA.
  • CIA (Certified Internal Auditor) — or commitment to obtain within 18 months.
  • 15–20 years in audit, risk or assurance, of which 5–7 years heading an internal audit division in an asset-heavy, multi-subsidiary group.
  • Prior BAC/Board reporting; track record of investigations.
  • Clean integrity record; passes MACC/regulatory background screening.

Strongly preferred

  • CFE (Certified Fraud Examiner), CISA or CRMA.
  • Ports, shipping, logistics, airports, utilities, oil & gas or construction exposure.
  • Major project / EPCC contract audit experience.
  • Implementation of data analytics / CCM in an audit function.
  • Experience with a listing, IPO readiness, or Bursa compliance.
  • MBA or Master's in Accounting/Governance.

Leadership competencies

  • Courage & independence of judgement.
  • Board-level presence and concise communication.
  • Commercial and operational acumen (“audits like an owner”).
  • Builds teams and methodology across distributed sites.
  • Bahasa Malaysia & English fluency; cultural agility with JV partners.

Headwinds / friction points

  • Frequent travel across Johor, Klang and Penang; site work at 24/7 operations.
  • Heavy multi-stakeholder scrutiny: Board, parent group, JV partners, port authorities, Customs, MACC.
  • Matrix complexity: group vs OpCo reporting and long-standing OpCo cultures.
  • Compensation compression vs Big Four partner and Singapore offers.
  • Perceived personal liability under s.17A and investigative pressure.
  • Small pool — 3-month notice periods and counter-offers are common.

Tailwinds / selling propositions

  • National-scale impact: guardian of Malaysia's maritime gateway and a top global transshipment hub (PTP).
  • Board-level visibility and direct line to the BAC Chair & Executive Chairman.
  • Mandate to build a modern, analytics-first audit function from a clean design.
  • Exposure to automation, smart-port and decarbonisation programmes.
  • Potential listing-related wealth creation through LTIP.
  • Career path to Group CRO, CFO, or CAE of the wider MMC group.
